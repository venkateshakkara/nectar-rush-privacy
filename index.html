Nectar Rush: Bee Maze – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Nectar Rush: Bee Maze ("the app") does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your best score on your device so it can show it the next time you play. This never leaves your device, and deleting the app removes it.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party services, SDKs, or advertising networks. No data is shared with anyone.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Nectar Rush: Bee Maze – Support

How to play

  • Swipe up, down, left, or right to steer the bee. On iPad with a keyboard, use the arrow keys or WASD.
  • Collect every grain of pollen to clear the level.
  • Avoid the spiders. Grab a pink blossom to scare them, then catch them for bonus points.
  • After Game Over, tap the screen to play again.

Frequently asked questions

Does the game need an internet connection?
No. It works completely offline.

How do I reset my best score?
Delete and reinstall the app.
